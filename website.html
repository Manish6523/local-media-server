VidLock visual identity
Serious playback

Whatever the file, just press play.

VidLock chooses the most efficient playback path automatically and remembers the details that matter.

Smart direct play

Browser-compatible video streams directly with HTTP Range requests—no unnecessary conversion.

Hardware transcoding

Unsupported formats become HLS using NVIDIA NVENC, VAAPI, Intel QSV, or a reliable CPU fallback.

Audio and subtitles

Switch between multiple audio tracks and use external or embedded subtitles right inside the player.

Continue watching

Progress updates while you watch, with automatic watched status after 90% and resume on your next visit.

Favorites and discovery

Keep a personal shortlist, search instantly, and move through movies and shows without losing your place.

Drive-aware library

Disconnected external drives are marked unavailable instead of deleting their carefully built library entries.
